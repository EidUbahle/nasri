Admissions

Begin your journey at Jigjiga University. We welcome students from all backgrounds.

Admission Requirements

  • Completed secondary education certificate
  • Minimum GPA of 2.5 for undergraduate programs
  • Pass entrance examination (for some programs)
  • English language proficiency (TOEFL/IELTS for international applicants)
  • Letters of recommendation (graduate applicants)

Application deadlines: Fall semester – June 30; Spring semester – November 15.

For detailed guidelines, contact the admissions office.
