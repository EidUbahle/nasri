About the Institute

Our mission, vision, and the values that drive us forward.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide high-quality technical education that fosters innovation, critical thinking, and ethical leadership in a rapidly evolving global landscape.

🔭

Our Vision

To be a world-class center of learning and research, producing graduates who drive technological advancement and contribute meaningfully to society.

🏛️

Our History

Established in 1985, the Institute of Technology has grown from a small polytechnic into a leading academic institution with over 12,000 students.

🤝

Our Values

Integrity, innovation, excellence, inclusivity, and community engagement are the cornerstones of our educational philosophy.

Start your journey at the Institute of Technology. Here's how to apply.

How to Apply

Our admissions process is designed to identify passionate and motivated students who are ready to excel in the field of technology. Follow these steps to complete your application.

  1. 1
    Review Requirements Check the prerequisites for your chosen program, including GPA and test scores.
  2. 2
    Submit Online Application Complete the form with your personal and academic details.
  3. 3
    Upload Documents Provide transcripts, recommendation letters, and a personal statement.
  4. 4
    Pay Application Fee A non-refundable fee of $75 is required to process your application.
  5. 5
    Attend Interview (if required) Some programs may require an online or in-person interview.

Important Dates: Applications open on January 15, 2026 and close on April 30, 2026.
